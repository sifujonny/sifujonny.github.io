Read before acting on any of this

  • No time trend is shown. Expired listings are already gone from this snapshot, so posting counts by date measure listing expiry, not market growth.
  • No cross-country salary comparison is shown. The currency column labels every row USD, but non-US figures track local currency.
  • Skills are not charted. 81% of postings have no skills recorded and descriptions are truncated at roughly 500 characters.
  • Role families were assigned by keyword rules on raw job titles. 14.4% could not be classified and are shown as “Other / Unclassified”.
